The Problem

A Compliance Bottleneck

The U.S. defense supply chain depends on thousands of small and mid-sized contract manufacturers—yet many can’t participate in aerospace and defense programs because qualifying for the necessary certifications is slow, expensive, and opaque.

To supply critical systems, manufacturers must comply with ITAR export controls and AS9100 quality management standards. For small shops, these are daunting. Compliance can cost tens of thousands of dollars and hundreds of staff hours each year—an unsustainable burden for a 50-person operation.

The result is a brittle industrial base concentrated among a few large suppliers, limiting the resilience of US-based supply chains.

Our Solution

Radical Efficiency. Real-Time Compliance.

We're building tools that automate documentation, simplify audits, and provide ongoing visibility into compliance status, allowing smaller firms to meet A&D requirements without needing in-house legal or quality teams.
