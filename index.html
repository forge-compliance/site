The Problem

The Compliance Barrier

Small contract manufacturers are frequently locked out of lucrative defense contracts due to complex and stringent CMMC 2.0 requirements. For shops with high-mix, low-volume catalogs, the administrative burden of tracking every part and process is overwhelming.

Existing consultants are expensive, slow, and disconnected from your day-to-day operations. Trying to handle it manually takes valuable engineering time away from the shop floor, stalling growth.

The result is missed opportunities and a restricted customer base, preventing capable manufacturers from scaling their business in the defense sector.

Our Solution

AI + Expert Support

Forge combines advanced AI technology with hands-on engineering support to streamline your path to compliance. We don't just help you check boxes; we integrate with your systems to turn compliance into a competitive advantage.

MRP Integration (Xometry)
Starting with Xometry, we integrate directly with your MRP system to pull project data automatically. No more manual entry—our system maps your existing workflows to compliance requirements in real-time.
AI Compliance Engine
Our AI analyzes your processes against CMMC 2.0 controls, generating required documentation and identifying gaps. It's a human-in-the-loop framework, ensuring accuracy with expert engineering oversight.
Growth & Sales Lead Generation
Forge isn't just a compliance tool; it's a sales engine. By achieving certification, you qualify for restricted defense contracts. We help you identify these opportunities and position your firm to win.
Engineering Support
Our team provides the hands-on engineering support you need to navigate technical requirements. We act as an extension of your team, ensuring your compliance journey is smooth and successful.
